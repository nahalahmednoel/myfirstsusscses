❔ How it works

HoodLust auto-mints on RobinHood Mainnet using the SeaDrop system.

Private Key Mode: Paste multiple private keys to automate minting across all your wallets simultaneously at the exact scheduled time without needing manual confirmation.

RPC Required: Make sure to enter your Alchemy RPC URL in Settings so the engine can broadcast transactions to the network.

Auto timing: The schedule is saved on this device. If a mint time is still in the future when you reopen, HoodLust re-arms it automatically.
