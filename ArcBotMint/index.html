How it works

HoodLust auto-mints on Arc Network (Chain ID: 5042) using the SeaDrop system via Private Keys.

Gas Fee Notice: On Arc Network, native transaction fees are paid in USDC.

Multi-Wallet Auto Mint: Paste multiple Private Keys in the Flash Mint window. When the timer hits 00:00:00, all wallets sign and mint automatically without needing individual manual approvals.

Auto timing: The schedule and keys persist locally. Keep the tab open near mint time for maximum speed.
